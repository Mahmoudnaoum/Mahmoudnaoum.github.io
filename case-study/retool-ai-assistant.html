Retool AI Assistant

A Growth Agency

Retool RAG AI Data

Problem

A growth agency had client data across their CRM, the ads warehouse, and a BigQuery analytics database. When account managers needed to answer a client question, they'd Slack the data team, wait for a query, then interpret the results. Simple questions like 'which campaigns drove the most revenue last month' took hours to answer.

What I Built

I built a Retool app with an AI assistant that runs RAG over their data. The team types a question in plain English, the LLM generates SQL, runs it against the warehouse and CRM, and returns the answer with a chart if it makes sense. Added guardrails so it can't write to the database or access tables outside the approved list. Logged every query so the data team can see what people are asking.

Result

Team asks questions in plain English, gets answers from their own data. Account managers can answer client questions during the call instead of saying 'I'll get back to you'. The data team sees the query log and knows which questions come up most often, so they can build those into dashboards. Questions that used to take half a day now take thirty seconds.
