Ad Spend + Product Data Warehouse

A UK Growth Agency

BigQuery Google Ads Meta Shopify GA4

Problem

A growth agency was running reports for their clients from five different places: Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Shopify admin, and GA4. Every Monday, someone spent hours exporting CSVs, copying numbers into a deck, and trying to make the metrics line up across platforms.

What I Built

I built a BigQuery data warehouse that pulls from all five sources on a schedule, joins them by client and campaign, and outputs one clean weekly board. Google Ads and Meta come in via API, Shopify via webhook, LinkedIn and GA4 through their connectors. Each client gets their own dataset. Spend, clicks, conversions, and revenue all in one query.

Result

One weekly board instead of five exports across three tools. The team opens one link on Monday morning and the numbers are already there, matched and ready. When a new client starts, they get added as a row in the config table and the pipeline picks them up automatically.
