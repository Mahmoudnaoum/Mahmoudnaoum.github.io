AI Weekly Brief Agent

A SaaS Company

n8n LLM Slack Automation

Problem

A SaaS company's head of growth spent two hours every Monday morning pulling numbers from PostHog, the ads warehouse, Stripe, and the CRM, then writing a summary for the exec team. By the time the update was ready, half the morning was gone and the meeting had usually started.

What I Built

I built an n8n workflow that runs Sunday night, queries all the data sources, feeds the numbers to an LLM with a prompt template, and posts the formatted summary to Slack. The prompt asks for week-over-week changes, flags anything that moved more than 15%, and keeps the tone conversational. If a data source fails, it posts an error and skips that section instead of breaking the whole brief.

Result

Monday summary auto-posted to Slack without manual reporting. The team opens Slack on Monday morning and the brief is already there. When MRR dropped one week, the alert fired and everyone saw it before the standup instead of discovering it mid-meeting. The head of growth gets those two hours back every week.
